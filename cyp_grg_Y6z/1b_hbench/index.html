Shipping to Cyprus

For a single piece like this, we currently do not have a provider that can ship by ocean as a less-than-container (LCL) consolidation to Cyprus. We’ve checked with two logistics partners, and both have confirmed this — Cyprus customs clearance on consolidated multi-customer containers is challenging.

That means this bench would need to go by DHL air courier — door-to-door, about 6 working days, but priced on chargeable weight (size + weight). Air freight costs naturally will be expensive.

At this time, to our knowledge, ocean freight is only possible when one customer fills a full container (FCL) under a single order — Cyprus clearance is workable in that case.
