What Happens Next

  1. You review this First Look

    This page is a starting look from one factory — enough to see whether the style and pricing generally feel right. Please share your feedback openly. If something doesn’t fit (look, size, or price), we’ll discuss it in a follow-up and adjust from there.

  2. Confirm direction & refine

    If you’re happy with the general direction, I suggest we move into a light formal engagement. As this is a small project, I’ll waive my usual US$500 project planning fee and ask only for a small nominal token fee — simply to confirm you’re serious about proceeding. We’ll then begin locking in what you like, change what needs changing, and approach one additional factory for alternate styles and comparative pricing on like pieces.

  3. Place the order

    Once selections and any custom specs are confirmed, we place the order — remotely or in person, whichever you prefer. Given the smaller size of this set and that you’re rather busy, a remote purchase can work well here. I’m happy to send extra photos or videos for confidence. You pay the factory directly for the goods. I arrange end-to-end logistics and charge logistics at cost. Total landed cost — including shipping fees and duties — is confirmed when the order is placed.
